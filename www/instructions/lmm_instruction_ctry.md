### Linear Regression Model Instructions (Country-level)

- Select one indicator as the outcome variable and one or more explanatory indicators (e.g. development measures, aggregated survey variables). 
- Estimate overall trends across countries (fixed effects) while allowing countries to vary in their baseline levels and trajectories (random effects). Examine model diagnostics, random effects, and predicted trajectories at both the global and country-specific levels.
- Interpret estimated effects, assess model assumptions using diagnostic plots, and compare predicted and observed country values.
