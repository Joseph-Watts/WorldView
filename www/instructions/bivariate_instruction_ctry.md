
#### Country-level bivariate summary statistics
- Uses country-level indicators from the Human Development Reports (HDRs) and aggregated WVS7 variables.
- The table reports descriptive measures for each variable and their relationship, supporting comparison and interpretation of associations across countries.
- Selects two variables and one or more countries to view summary statistics describing their joint distribution at the country level.
- Allows comparisons between HDR × HDR indicators, or HDR × WVS aggregated indicators.
