## Welcome!
<br><br>

This website is for PSYC382 Cognition and Culture students at the University of Canterbury.

This page allows you to visualize data from [Wave 7 (2017-2022) of the World Valued Survey](https://www.worldvaluessurvey.org/WVSDocumentationWV7.jsp). 
The data used on this website is subject to copyright and used here only for educational purposes. 

This page has been made with [shiny](https://shiny.rstudio.com/) using [this code](https://github.com/abenedetti/bioNPS/) as a base.

If there are issues with the functioning of this website, please contact your TA.
