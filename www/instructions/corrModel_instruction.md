## Correlations

Select two survey questions to assess the correlation between them.

Available correlation methods include Pearson, Kendall, and Spearman.

- Use Pearson when both variables are numeric and you want to assess the strength of a linear relationship.
- Use Spearman when the variables are ordinal, not normally distributed, or when the relationship may be monotonic but not strictly linear.
- Use Kendall when working with ordinal variables or smaller samples, especially when you want a more conservative measure of rank association.

Missing data (NA) are removed from the data used in this analysis.
