#### Group-level Scatter Plot

-   Shows the relationship between two selected variables at the group level.
-   Each point represents a group of countries.
-   The position of a point reflects official UNDP-reported aggregate values for the selected X and Y variables.
-   Variables can be selected from the Human Development Reports (HDR) and the World Values Survey Wave 7 (WVS7), allowing exploration of broader social, economic, and development patterns across groups of countries.
