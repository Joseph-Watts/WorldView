# Frequently Asked Questions

<br>

## What is the source of the data?

The WVS is an international research program that investigates people’s values, beliefs, and cultural attitudes across different countries.<br> All data used in this app comes from the [**World Values Survey - Wave 7**](https://www.worldvaluessurvey.org/WVSDocumentationWV7.jsp).

This specific dataset was conducted between 2017 and 2022 and is the most recent completed wave.<br>

The majority of surveys were completed in 2018-2020 with only about a dozen of countries conducting their fieldwork since the pandemic outbreak in 2021-2022. The last included survey comes from India and was completed in July 2023.

Wave 8 started in January 2024. WVS-8 is planned for 2024-2026<br>

Waves 1 through 6 (1981 to 2014) are also available to download, however that data is not loaded in this app currently.<br>

<br>

## What transformations have been made to the data?

For educational purposes, minor transformations may have been applied to the data, such as coding responses, filtering for specific variables, or aggregating data to facilitate analysis. However, no major alterations have been made to the dataset that would impact its representativeness or validity as per the original survey's design.

<br>

## Are the samples representative of each country?

As per the WVS rules, every country is surveyed once per wave. All countries employed random probability representative samples of the adult population.<br> Vast majority of surveys were conducted using face-to-face interview (PAPI/CAPI) as the data collection mode.

The WVS aims to provide nationally representative samples for each participating country. Samples are carefully selected to reflect the demographics of each country’s population, including factors such as age, gender, and regional distribution. This representativeness allows researchers to make more accurate cross-national comparisons.

<br>

## Were all countries included in the survey?

No, not all countries are included in each wave of the WVS. Wave 7 covers a broad range of countries, but participation depends on various factors, including logistical, financial, and political considerations. In total, Wave 7 includes data from over 70 countries and regions, representing a significant portion of the world’s population but not encompassing every nation.
