WorldView phylogeny attribution update
====================================

Copy the contents of this package into the ROOT of your current WorldView
repository, preserving the folder structure, and replace the existing files.

REPLACE:
  ATTRIBUTION.md
  worldview_deployment/assets/step28-enhancements.js

This update adds the phylogeny source attribution in two visible locations:

1. Visualisations > Phylogeny
2. About > Sources

It also adds a dedicated "Phylogeny" section to ATTRIBUTION.md.

Citation used:

Bouckaert, R., Redding, D., Sheehan, O., Kyritsis, T., Gray, R., Jones, K. E.,
& Atkinson, Q. (2022, 1 July). Global language diversification is linked to
socio-ecology and threat status. doi:10.31235/osf.io/f8tr6

The title links to:
https://doi.org/10.31235/osf.io/f8tr6

Testing:
1. Run:
     servr::httd("worldview_deployment")
2. Hard-refresh with Ctrl+F5.
3. Open Visualisations > Phylogeny and confirm the citation appears.
4. Open About > Sources and confirm the citation appears there too.
5. Click the citation title and confirm the DOI opens.

No WVS data rebuild is required.
