# WorldView WVS Wave 7 Codebook

This codebook describes the processed variables displayed in 
    the WorldView Shiny app. Original negative missing and 
    non-response codes are simplified to `NA`. The values displayed here
    are the processed values used by this app

The origional WVS codebook and data documentation for the Wave 7 
    can be [found here](https://www.worldvaluessurvey.org/WVSDocumentationWV7.jsp).

Please note that the WorldView app displays only a small subset of
    variable from the WVS for teaching purposes.

**Question:** In which of the following things do you believe, if you believe in any? - God

**Processed data type:** numeric

**Processed values**

- 1 = Yes

### Q275: Highest educational level of respondent
**Question:** What is the highest educational level that you have attained?

**Processed data type:** ordered numeric in indiv_ordinal; ordered factor in intermediate indiv

**Processed values**
- 1 = Early childhood education / no education
- 2 = Primary education
- 3 = Lower
- 4 = Lower secondary education
- 5 = Middle
- 6 = Upper secondary education
- 7 = Higher
- 8 = Post-secondary non-tertiary education
- 9 = Short-cycle tertiary education
- 10 = Bachelor or equivalent
- 11 = Master or equivalent
- 12 = Doctoral or equivalent

**Processing note:** Haven labelled values converted to an ordered factor, then ordered factors are converted to ordinal integers with negative missing codes set to NA.
