## Histogram Visualization 

Visualize the distribution of responses for a selected survey question.

Compare distributions across countries using histograms or density plots. You can adjust the number of bins, add normal curves, and choose between faceted or overlaid displays.

Factor variables are converted into numeric values based on their order in the Codebook, with numbering starting at 1. For example, No = 1 and Yes = 2.

Missing data (NA) are removed from the data used here.
